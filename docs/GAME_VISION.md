# ALSAEQA — Game Vision

## Identity
- Arabic title: الصاعقة
- English identity: ALSAEQA
- Genre: cinematic third-person 3D action-adventure
- Engine: Unreal Engine 5
- Code: C++ with Blueprint gameplay composition
- Primary platform: Android

## Story

The protagonist begins as a poor man forced into brutal labor by enslavers. During a catastrophic collapse beneath a mine, he discovers an ancient symbol connected to his bloodline. Contact with the symbol awakens supernatural thunder power and reveals that he is descended from the ancient Thunder Giants.

He is the last hidden heir. His family was hunted because their ability to command natural forces threatened powerful enemies. He leaves the mines and begins a long exploration journey through forests, caves, mountains, ruins, deserts, castles, frozen lands, and the lost homeland of the giants.

Along the journey he frees a woman who endured the same oppression. She becomes a permanent companion with her own abilities, history, secrets, equipment, and character arc.

## World

The world is designed as an expandable connected adventure rather than a fixed small set of stages.

Initial regions:
1. Slave Mines
2. Shadow Forest
3. Thunder Mountains
4. Cursed Caves
5. Monster Valley
6. Abandoned City
7. Tyrant's Castle
8. Black Desert
9. Ice Kingdom
10. Land of Giants

Each region supports exploration, combat, traversal, puzzles, secrets, treasures, environmental storytelling, unique creatures, and bosses.

## Hero powers

- Thunder Shock — ranged electrical strike.
- Lightning Dash — rapid traversal and combat movement.
- Thunder Jump — charged vertical leap.
- Storm Fist — close-range thunder attack.
- Lightning Shield — defensive electrical barrier.
- Storm Summon — large-area storm ability.
- Thunder Eye — reveals hidden paths, secrets, enemies and treasures.
- Thunder Echo — interacts with ancient thunder mechanisms and reveals traces of the past.

Abilities are progression tools as well as combat tools. New powers should unlock traversal routes, puzzle solutions, secrets and environmental interactions.

## Combat

Core weapons include sword, dagger, spear, hammer and an evolving thunder weapon. Combat should reward positioning, timing, dodging, blocking, counters, powers and enemy-specific tactics rather than repetitive button mashing.

## Traversal

The hero can climb and traverse rocks, mountains, walls, trees, towers, caves and ruins. Certain routes require powers, equipment, weapons, environmental interaction or puzzle completion.

## Creatures

The world contains predators, small fast enemies, cave monsters, forest creatures, flying creatures, giants, thunder creatures and major bosses. Bosses use distinct mechanics rather than simple high health.

## Environment

Vegetation reacts to wind and movement. Water, fog, fire, dust, storms and lighting contribute to the atmosphere. The world should feel alive through ambient wildlife, environmental motion, weather variation and reactive effects.

## Camera

Third-person 3D camera with full look-around, vertical look, controlled zoom, combat framing and cinematic camera sequences.

## Quality strategy

The game targets multiple scalability tiers so Android devices can use appropriate rendering and effects quality. High-cost features must not be mandatory for the baseline mobile experience. Performance, memory, thermal behavior and battery usage are first-class requirements.
