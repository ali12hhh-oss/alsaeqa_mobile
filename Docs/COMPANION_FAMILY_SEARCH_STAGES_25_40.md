# Companion Family Search — Stages 25–40

## Permanent rule
The permanent companion's active family search begins at **stage 25** and continues as a major parallel story arc through **stage 40**.

Before stage 25, family material remains clues, memories, objects, symbols and testimony only. It must never become an active family-search objective.

## Search structure
The player does not receive the whole answer at stage 25. Instead, every group of stages adds a stronger piece of evidence and may redirect the investigation.

### Stage 25 — The First Confirmed Trail
- Convert accumulated clues into the first credible family trail.
- Companion chooses to actively search.
- Activate family-search objectives and map markers.
- Find an old convoy record, route mark or sanctuary reference.
- Establish that the family was deliberately scattered rather than simply lost.

### Stage 26 — Abandoned Convoy
- Investigate the first confirmed route.
- Search an abandoned convoy site.
- Recover personal objects and travel records.
- Discover evidence that prisoners were transferred instead of killed together.

### Stage 27 — The Broken Mark
- Follow a damaged family symbol across a dangerous route.
- Companion recognizes part of the symbol from childhood.
- The clue identifies a second destination but leaves uncertainty about who survived.

### Stage 28 — Witness in Hiding
- Locate a frightened former captive or traveler.
- Protect the witness during an enemy attack.
- The witness confirms that at least one family member may have survived the original transfer.

### Stage 29 — The Prison Ledger
- Infiltrate or investigate an enemy holding site.
- Recover a ledger containing names, aliases or transfer codes.
- One entry matches the companion's family trail.

### Stage 30 — The Split Path
- The evidence points to two possible destinations.
- The companion must evaluate which trail is more credible.
- The wrong-looking trail can still reveal useful information; the search must not permanently dead-end.

### Stage 31 — The Hidden Shelter
- Explore a concealed shelter used by displaced families.
- Find childhood belongings or a familiar craft/mark.
- Discover that someone protected the companion's family after the original capture.

### Stage 32 — The Survivor Message
- Recover a message left for a family member.
- The message is incomplete and may be intentionally coded.
- Companion gains a clearer understanding of the final instructions she remembered from childhood.

### Stage 33 — Enemy Network
- Connect several records to the organization that transported the family.
- Reveal that the family was valuable to the enemy for reasons separate from the hero's Thunder Giant bloodline.
- Keep the two family mysteries distinct.

### Stage 34 — The Mountain Route
- Follow evidence into a dangerous mountain passage.
- Use exploration, traversal and companion abilities to reach an isolated location.
- Find signs of a later movement by one or more survivors.

### Stage 35 — The False Grave
- Investigate a location believed to be the final resting place of a family member.
- Discover that the evidence is misleading or incomplete.
- The companion learns not to treat absence of evidence as proof of death.

### Stage 36 — The Second Convoy
- Recover a later transfer record.
- Establish a chain of locations rather than a single destination.
- The search becomes a continuing pursuit of a moving trail.

### Stage 37 — The Survivor Route
- Follow the strongest surviving lead.
- Discover a safe route or coded signs left by survivors.
- Companion begins to believe the family story can be resolved without guaranteeing a reunion.

### Stage 38 — The Family Stronghold
- Reach a heavily protected location connected to the family.
- Investigate, infiltrate or fight through the area.
- Recover the most substantial collection of evidence so far.

### Stage 39 — The Truth Behind the Separation
- Assemble the evidence collected since stage 25.
- Reveal why the family was separated and what happened to the known members.
- Resolve contradictions in the childhood memories.
- Leave room for a final confirmation rather than forcing an unrealistic complete reunion.

### Stage 40 — The Family Truth
- Complete the active search arc's main investigation.
- The companion receives the clearest available answer about her family.
- Any surviving family members, if established by the accumulated evidence, become story consequences rather than a magically convenient resolution.
- Preserve unresolved threads when they serve the larger adventure.
- The family-search arc may intersect with the hero's lineage only after both stories have independently developed.

## Evidence rules
Every evidence item collected from stage 25 onward should have a unique persistent ID and be saved permanently.

Evidence categories:
- Personal object
- Written record
- Survivor testimony
- Family symbol
- Convoy/transfer record
- Childhood memory confirmation
- Enemy record
- Safe-route marking
- Location evidence
- Survivor confirmation

Duplicate evidence must not increase the count twice.

## Stage progression rules
- Search begins only at stage 25.
- Search remains active through stage 40.
- Evidence can be collected in revisitable areas.
- The player may discover optional evidence in a different order from the main stage order.
- Advancing the main stage must never delete previously collected evidence.
- Returning to an old region can reveal new family evidence after the appropriate stage.
- Capture and rescue of the companion can occur during this arc without resetting family progress.
- The family arc is separate from the hero's Thunder Giant ancestry arc until a late, earned intersection.

## Player experience
The player should feel like they are conducting a real investigation: finding records, comparing clues, protecting witnesses, exploring old routes, recognizing symbols, following transfers and revisiting locations with new knowledge.

The answer should be earned gradually from stages 25–40, not delivered through one dialogue scene.
