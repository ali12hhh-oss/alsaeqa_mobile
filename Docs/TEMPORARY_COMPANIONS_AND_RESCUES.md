# Temporary Companions and Rescue Adventures

The hero can temporarily travel with rescued allies. Each ally has a role, personality, useful gameplay ability, a destination, and a meaningful departure. Groups of prisoners can also be rescued and escorted through hostile territory.

## Companion examples

- Nadir: scout who marks patrols and hidden paths.
- Mira: archer who can hit distant rope targets.
- Khaled: fighter who protects rescued workers during escape.
- Sana: climber who stabilizes traversal ropes.

## Rescue escalation

Rescues progress from opening a cell to multi-objective escapes: scout guards, disable alarms, free prisoners, survive reinforcements, protect the group, cross a dangerous route, and reach a safe location.

## Connected stages

Every stage leaves a clue for the next. A rescued prisoner can identify a symbol, a freed group can establish a later settlement, a defeated boss can leave a map fragment, and a family relic can reveal a route that only a later power can access.

## Giant threats

Giant snakes, stone colossi, thunder beasts, giant hunters, and chained giants appear progressively. Their encounters use terrain, grabs, area attacks, climbing pressure, and environmental hazards instead of simple high health.
