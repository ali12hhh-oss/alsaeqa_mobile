# ALSAEQA — Expanded Adventure Stages

This is the continuous progression plan for the game. There are no V1/V2/V3 splits. New mechanics are discovered naturally through the same adventure.

## Canonical story authority
The hero's origin, identity, childhood events, mine awakening and Stage 1 story are governed by `HERO_ORIGIN_AND_MAIN_STORY.md`. If another roadmap or cinematic note conflicts with that document, the canonical hero story takes precedence and the conflicting note must be reconciled before implementation.

The permanent companion is first met and rescued in Stage 5. Her active family search remains locked until Stage 25 and continues through Stage 40 according to the companion family documents.

## New progression after the first nine stages

### Stage 10 — The Storm Thread
- New ability: **Thunder Thread** — a short-range electrical tether that pulls light objects, activates distant switches, and can rescue the hero from selected falls.
- Adventure: follow lightning scars through a canyon, solve a three-part current puzzle, and defeat a cliff guardian.
- New exploration language: tether points become visible only through Thunder Eye.

### Stage 11 — The Hunter of the Mist
- New weapon: **Storm Bow**.
- Adventure: track a legendary hunter through the Shadow Forest, recover three broken bow parts, and defeat an enemy that moves only inside fog.
- Combat addition: ranged weak-point attacks and silent takedowns.

### Stage 12 — The Living Mountain
- New ability: **Thunder Grip** — temporarily magnetizes the hero to marked stone and metal surfaces.
- Adventure: climb a moving mountain face during an electrical storm.
- New traversal: wall-to-wall jumps, emergency grip, slips, and companion-assisted rescues.

### Stage 13 — The Chain of the Colossus
- New weapon: **Colossus Chain**.
- Adventure: enter an underground prison, free chained creatures, and take the weapon from a massive guardian after a multi-stage arena encounter.
- Combat addition: pull enemies, interrupt attacks, and swing across marked anchors.

### Stage 14 — The Storm Within
- New ability: **Overcharge**.
- The player can temporarily amplify an equipped power, but overuse causes instability and forces a recovery window.
- Adventure: cross a lightning field while managing charge level and ancient stabilizers.

### Stage 15 — The Fallen City
- New weapon: **Thunder Axe**.
- Adventure: explore an abandoned city whose streets change during storms. Find the hidden forge, recover its missing core, and defeat the forge guardian.
- New world feature: environmental events can temporarily alter traversal routes.

### Stage 16 — The Companion's Secret
- The permanent female companion receives her own major story arc.
- She discovers a connection to the people who hunted the Thunder Giants.
- New companion mechanic: command her to open routes, distract enemies, use her weapon, and solve paired puzzles.
- Her choices affect dialogue, combat assistance, and optional discoveries, while keeping the main story coherent.

### Stage 17 — The Black Desert
- New ability: **Storm Veil** — briefly reduces detection and allows movement through dangerous electrical sandstorms.
- New enemies: burrowing predators and storm scavengers.
- New survival loop: read wind direction, shelter behind ruins, and move between safe windows.

### Stage 18 — The Frozen Kingdom
- New weapon: **Frostbreaker Spear**.
- The weapon is not simply stronger; it counters specific frozen creatures and can create temporary traversal anchors.
- Boss: a chained ice titan with armor that must be broken through environmental interactions.

### Stage 19 — The Echo of the Ancestors
- New ability: **Thunder Memory**.
- Ancient echoes reconstruct fragments of the past at specific locations.
- The player can observe historical events, learn enemy weaknesses, and discover optional family paths without turning the game into exposition-only scenes.

### Stage 20 — The Land of Giants
- The hero reaches the first true Thunder Giant sanctuary.
- New weapon: **Heir's Blade**.
- The weapon only awakens after the player completes a sequence of family trials rather than simply finding it in a chest.
- Major revelation: the hero's family was not destroyed for power alone; they were protecting a natural force capable of changing the world.

## Systems that keep later stages exciting

1. **Reactive world events** — storms, cave-ins, animal migrations, enemy patrols, fires, floods, and collapsing routes can create short unscripted encounters.
2. **Layered discoveries** — Thunder Eye reveals clues; Thunder Thread reaches them; Thunder Memory explains them; later weapons can unlock their physical mechanisms.
3. **Dedicated weapon adventures** — every major weapon has a distinct expedition, guardian, acquisition scene, and mastery challenge.
4. **Power mastery trials** — every new ability gets optional challenges that teach combat, traversal, and puzzle applications.
5. **Escalating boss design** — bosses gain new behavior and environmental phases rather than only more health.
6. **Companion rescue system** — the companion can save the hero in selected traversal situations, while the hero can also rescue her.
7. **Near-death cinematics** — selected falls, perfect dodges, parries, rescues, and boss attacks can trigger short controlled cinematic moments.
8. **Secret family routes** — optional paths contain relics, lore, cosmetics, upgrade materials, and alternate approaches rather than mandatory exposition.
9. **No early power dump** — the player never starts with the full ability or weapon set. The adventure earns every major capability.
10. **Continuous world** — regions remain part of the same journey and can become revisitable as new powers change what is possible.

## Asset-production rule

The final game must use the project's supplied production-grade real character, environment, animation, audio, VFX and authored Unreal assets. **Primitive geometry is not an acceptable final visual substitute.** Runtime procedural or built-in primitive geometry may exist only as an internal development/fallback foundation where unavoidable; it must never be represented as the completed art direction, final cinematic content, or final gameplay presentation when the corresponding real asset exists.
