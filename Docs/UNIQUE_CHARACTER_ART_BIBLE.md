# ALSAEQA — Unique Character Art Bible

This document defines the final visual identity. Generic stock characters are not the final result. Licensed meshes may be used only as production bases and must be reconfigured into distinct ALSAEQA characters through silhouette, outfit, hair, materials, equipment, proportions, accessories and animation selection.

## Hero — وريث الرعد
- Male protagonist; lean athletic silhouette, not a default knight.
- Distinct asymmetrical survivor outfit: worn work clothing evolving into ancient thunder armor.
- Signature shoulder/arm protection on one side, wrapped forearm, utility belt and mining-origin details.
- Short-to-medium distinctive hairstyle; no helmet covering the face during story scenes.
- Weapon silhouette: one recognizable thunder-forged blade with a non-generic guard.
- Progression: worker -> awakened heir -> storm warrior -> Thunder Giant descendant.
- Materials: weathered cloth/leather/metal with controlled lightning emissive accents.
- No permanent glowing body. Lightning appears during abilities and high-charge states.

## Companion — الرفيقة
- Female protagonist with a clearly different silhouette and fighting stance.
- Layered travel outfit, light armor and a distinctive family-symbol accessory.
- Different hairstyle and color/material palette from Hero.
- Signature paired weapon or light polearm; never the Hero's sword duplicated.
- Her costume gains small visual changes as her family-search story advances from stage 25 to 40.
- Capture/rescue states use damaged/dirtied variants, then restored appearance after rescue.

## Humanoid enemies
Create visually distinct factions rather than recolors:
1. Mine Overseers — industrial armor, lamps, hooks/tools, heavy silhouettes.
2. Tyrant Soldiers — disciplined armor, shields and polearms.
3. Storm Hunters — specialized anti-thunder gear, coils/insulators and ranged weapons.
4. Fortress Elite — heavier ornate armor and large silhouettes.
5. Desert Raiders — cloth, masks, curved weapons and light equipment.
6. Ice Guard — layered winter armor and frozen-metal weapons.

Each faction receives at least three silhouette variants and multiple equipment combinations.

## Bosses
Every boss must have a unique silhouette and a dedicated weapon/attack language. Do not create a boss by simply scaling an ordinary enemy. Boss presentation requires unique mesh combinations, materials, armor, weapon, animation montages, hit reactions and phase-specific VFX.

## Giant Snake
- Dedicated full creature mesh with continuous body, head, jaw, eyes, fangs and authored scales/material.
- No chain of primitive ellipsoids in final gameplay.
- Animations: idle, coil, crawl, slither, turn, ambush, bite, charge, retreat, hurt, death.
- Venom presentation uses material/VFX and animation timing rather than primitive geometry.

## Mounts
- Horse: realistic anatomical proportions and tack/saddle.
- Beast: large quadruped with heavier armor and distinct gait.
- ThunderBeast: unique creature identity, horns/crest, thunder-reactive materials and dedicated special ability animations.
- FlyingBeast: dedicated winged skeletal mesh, takeoff, flight, landing and aerial turns.
- Mounted hero uses separate rider animations where available.

## Weapons
Each weapon family has its own authored mesh silhouette and material treatment. Never attach a generic cylinder as a final weapon.
- Thunder blade
- Companion weapon
- Mine tools
- Soldier polearms
- Shields
- Boss weapons
- ThunderBeast harness/armor

## Animation standard
Imported skeletal animation is mandatory for final characters. Required sets include locomotion, start/stop, turns, jumps, falls, landings, attacks, combos, hit reactions, dodge/parry, ability montages, death and contextual interaction animations. Procedural transform oscillation is development fallback only.

## Art uniqueness rule
A reused source mesh is acceptable only as a starting production asset. Final characters must be distinguishable at thumbnail size by silhouette and equipment. Recolor-only variants do not count as unique characters.

## Performance rule
Keep meshes game-ready for Android scalability. Use LODs, texture resolution appropriate to device tier, material instances, skeletal mesh optimization and animation compression. High-quality presentation must not require desktop-only rendering features.

## No religious imagery
The world is fantasy/adventure and must not introduce mosques, churches or other religious buildings as generic decoration.
